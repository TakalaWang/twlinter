# Code of Conduct

TWLinter is a collaborative open-source project. Participants are expected to
be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different backgrounds and
levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and disruptive conduct.

Report a concern privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers
may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed
to protect the project community.
